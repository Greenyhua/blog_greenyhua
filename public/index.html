About Me

Hi👋, I am Shijia Qing, a Master’s student in Applied Psychology at Sun Yat-sen University, under the supervision of Professor Guomei, Zhou. My reseach interests are human-computer interaction, social cognition, and the uncanny valley effect.

✨My research explores how the concept of self and social familiarity shape perceptions of humanoid robots and artificial agents🤖.
✨I have also contributed to the development of theoretical frameworks and measurement tools🖥️, including meta-analytic approaches and scale validation studies.

📖Alongside my academic pursuits, I interned at Seasun Games and NetEase, where I conducted user experience research, competitive analysis, and playtesting for videogames🎮.

🏃‍♀️I am eager to further explore human-computer interaction and contribute to advancing our understanding of how humans interact with intelligent systems!
